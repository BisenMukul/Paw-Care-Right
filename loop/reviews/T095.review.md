## F1 — FIXED, and the methodology is the real lhci semantic

I read `@lhci/utils`'s implementation myself rather than taking the note's word for it:
`node_modules/.pnpm/@lhci+utils@0.14.0/node_modules/@lhci/utils/src/representative-runs.js` sorts the
