### F1 — MEDIUM — Two source URLs in §3 were synthesised, not transcribed

This directly contradicts the doc's own §3 preamble at `:121-123` ("Every row below is transcribed from the dossier") and §1 at `:31-35` ("No fact in this document was invented, inferred, or recalled"). Not HIGH because no verdict, status, or clearance claim is affected — but in a deliverable whose entire value is provenance discipline, an invented citation is the wrong kind of mistake.

**Recommended fix (executor, 2 lines):** replace both URL cells with the dossier's literal query text, e.g. `WebSearch: "Nuzzo" OR "Pawnest" trademark registered (no stable URL — search-engine result)`.
