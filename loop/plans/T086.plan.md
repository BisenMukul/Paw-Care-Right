## Key decisions (restated as risks at the end)

- **D1 — `LEGAL-REVIEW` marker mechanism = one shared component rendering a *visible* banner whose
  text starts with the literal token `LEGAL-REVIEW`, plus a `data-legal-review="<topic>"` attribute
  and `data-testid="legal-review-marker"`.** Rejected: an HTML comment (JSX cannot emit comments
  without `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` — adding an injection surface just to hide a marker is
  indefensible, and a comment is invisible to a human reviewer, which is the point of the marker).
  Rejected: a code-only constant (not greppable in the shipped HTML, so AC2's "legal pages contain
  review markers" would be unverifiable on the artefact). The chosen form is greppable in **source**
  and in **built HTML**, and visible to the human doing the C3 pass.
- **D2 — Deep-link scope = a scheme-link builder + one honest "Open in the app" affordance on the
  landing page. NO `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association`, NO `assetlinks.json`, NO `/open`
  route.** Reasons, all verifiable: (i) an AASA file requires a real Apple Team ID and `assetlinks`
  requires the release keystore's SHA-256 fingerprint — neither exists; writing either means
  **fabricating identifiers**, which is exactly what §1a/C3 forbids while bundle ids are
  provisional; (ii) `apps/mobile/app.config.js` declares no `associatedDomains`/`intentFilters`, so
  a web-side association file would be one-sided and non-functional — an artefact that *looks*
  shipped but does nothing; (iii) mobile exposes only `join/:code` and `checks/:id` deep links,
  both of which need a private token a marketing page cannot mint; (iv) adding an `/open` route
  would add a route to `app/sitemap.ts` and break T085's `3 + FOOD_PAGE_COUNT` assertion for no
  user benefit. What ships is real: `pawcareright://` built from `DEEPLINK_SCHEME`, with honest
  fallback copy for the (currently universal) not-installed case. Universal-link association files
  are handed off to **T099 (store setup) / C3** in the journal.
- **D3 — No route additions, no `app/sitemap.ts` change, no `app/layout.tsx` change.** The three
  marketing pages each export their own `metadata` (title/description/canonical). Keeps T085's
  sitemap-count and SEO contract tests untouched.
- **D4 — Site chrome (footer) is rendered by the three marketing pages only, not by
  `app/layout.tsx`.** Putting a footer in the root layout would change the built HTML of all 466
  T085 pages (their `<a>`-text/`<img>`-alt/`noindex` contract tests would then police my markup)
  for zero acceptance value. Legal links on food pages are noted as a T098/T095 hand-off.
- **D5 — Presentational split mirrors T085 (D4 there):** all markup lives in *synchronous*
  components under `src/components/{marketing,legal}/**` taking a plain model; `app/**/page.tsx`
  files are thin. This is what makes the whole surface testable with `renderToStaticMarkup` and
  zero new dependencies.
- **D6 — The landing carries a 6-link "popular food-safety answers" strip** into T085's pages.
  Justification (not decoration): the 466 programmatic pages currently have **no inbound internal
  link** — the site's only entry point links to nothing, which is both an SEO defect and the exact
  reason AC3 says "links validated". Bounded to 6 dataset-derived links, each asserted to exist in
  `allFoodPagePaths()`. It is **not** a hub page (that stays out of scope).
- **D7 — Pricing phrasing = "planned pricing", USD anchors from SPEC §7, no purchase affordance.**
  Pinned notice below. Regional pricing is mentioned as "prices differ by country".
- **D8 — Legal copy lives in `src/strings.ts`** (CLAUDE §6 "no hardcoded user-facing strings in
  components", i18n-ready for T110); document *structure* (section order, ids, review topics) lives
  in `src/legal/*-document.ts`. Paragraphs that name the product are
  `(appName: string) => string` and are resolved by the builder — never a hardcoded name (T085's
  `render.spec.tsx:349` walk already enforces this for every new `src` file).

**Privacy content requirements (executor writes the prose; these are the testable obligations).**
Every section has ≥1 paragraph of ≥120 characters. `pet-health-data` states that symptom
descriptions, photos and health entries are sensitive and are processed to produce guidance;
`ai-guidance` states that intake text and photos are sent to AI providers to generate guidance,
that guidance is general and not veterinary care, and that photo EXIF metadata is stripped on
upload; `sharing-and-processors` lists the vendor categories the constitution locks in (AI
providers, analytics, error reporting, subscription/payments, push delivery, object storage) and
carries the `sub-processors` marker; `retention` describes retention while the account is active
plus deletion on request via `privacy@pawcareright.app` (**H3**: no claim of an in-app delete
button — T091 is unbuilt); `household-sharing` states plainly that every household member can see
shared pets, reminders and timeline entries; `gdpr` lists the EU/EEA/UK rights (access,
rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority);
`ccpa` covers California rights (know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing) with an explicit
"we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising"
statement; `children` states the service is not directed to children under 13.

**Terms content requirements.** `subscriptions-and-billing` states that subscriptions are billed by
